The {{ui:Starter library}} gives you everyday things at real sizes, so a model reads at a glance: a door to show the scale of a room, a car in a driveway, people and trees around a building.

## Place one

1. In {{ui:Components}} on the left, click {{ui:Starter library}}.
2. Pick a thing, such as {{ui:Chair}}.
3. Click where it goes.

On a floor, a table or the ground, it stands on it. **Doors and windows** are made to go in walls: click on a wall and they stand in it, facing out. (They sit on the wall's face; they do not cut an opening. To make one, draw a rectangle on the wall where the window goes and push it through.)

Each thing is a **component**: picking the same one again places another copy, and every copy changes if you edit one. It also shows in the {{ui:Components}} list, with {{ui:Place}} to add more.

## What is in it

| Group | Things |
|---|---|
| Doors and windows | Door, double door, window, wide window, garage door |
| Furniture | Dining table, round table, chair, bar stool, sofa, armchair, double and single bed, desk, bookshelf, wardrobe |
| Kitchen and bath | Base and wall cabinet, kitchen counter, fridge, stove, sink unit, toilet, bathtub, shower, washbasin |
| Building | Straight stair (15 risers of 18 cm), round and square column, beam |
| Site and people | Tree, shrub, person (1.75 m), car |

They are simple shapes, made of solids you can paint, scale or [make unique](topic:groups-components) and change. The wall cabinet hangs at 1.45 m above where you click.
